Privacy Policy for MyPay

Last updated: October 4, 2026

MyPay ("we", "our", or "us") is a Pi Network application that allows users to send and receive Pi payments.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum information necessary to operate the app:

  • Your Pi Network username
  • Your Pi Wallet address (when you make or receive payments)
  • Payment transaction data related to MyPay

We do not collect personal information such as your real name, email, phone number, or location unless you voluntarily provide it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process Pi payments
  • To display your payment history inside the app
  • To improve the app experience

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Payment data is processed through the official Pi Network SDK and blockchain.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data by contacting us through the app or stopping use of MyPay.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact: For any questions, please contact us through the MyPay app.
